Open csvTV offline

Use a regular Safari tab on your iPhone. This test puts a fictional episode of Signal Garden in the Library on this device.

  1. Tap the button below while online. In Settings, wait for Ready to open offline.
  2. Open Library and bookmark that page. Turn on Airplane Mode and turn off Wi-Fi.
  3. Close the tracker tab. Open a new Safari tab and use the bookmark. Check that Signal Garden appears with one watched episode.
  4. Open the show, change its rating, and return to Library. Close the tab and reopen the bookmark again. Check that the new rating is there, then reconnect.
